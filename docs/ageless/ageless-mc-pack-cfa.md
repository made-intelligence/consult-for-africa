## What to say

### 6:30 Welcome

Good evening, ladies and gentlemen, and welcome to AGELESS.

My name is Olive Emodi, and it is my pleasure to be your host tonight.

AGELESS is an evening on a simple question: how do we look good, feel well and live well as we age? Tonight you will hear from doctors and practitioners who work on that question every day, from longevity medicine and metabolism to fitness, stress and skin. You should leave with things you can do this week, not just ideas.

Tonight is hosted by MedLYFE Wellness and Longevity Centre and The Lyfe Place.

A few quick notes. Please put your phones on silent. Photographs are welcome during the programme, but please no flash. There will be time for your questions after the panel, and a microphone will come to you. And after the programme, stay with us for Ageless After Hours, when the clinicians and speakers will be in the room to talk with you.

To begin, a short film.

### 6:40 Introducing Dr Ajelabi

Our opening address asks the question behind the whole evening: can we live to 100 and still live well?

Dr Adedotun Ajelabi is Head of Medicals at MedLYFE Wellness and Longevity Centre. She is a Consultant Family Physician and a Fellow of the West African College of Physicians, with specialist training in longevity medicine through the American Board of Longevity Medicine. Her work is about moving healthcare from treating disease to preventing it.

Please welcome Dr Adedotun Ajelabi.

When she finishes: Thank you, Dr Ajelabi.

### 7:00 Introducing the panel

Now to the heart of the evening, our panel: The Ageless Blueprint, practical steps to look good, feel well and live well as you age.

Please welcome to the stage:

- **Dr Timi Adenuga**, Lead Bariatric and Laparoscopic Surgeon at GetSlim Nigeria, who has performed more than a thousand weight-loss procedures. He speaks on weight, metabolism and longevity.
- **Gbemi Giwa**, award-winning fitness and nutrition coach and founder of the African Fat Loss Method. She speaks on lifestyle and behaviour change.
- **Joycee Awosika**, founder and Managing Director of the ORÍKÌ Group, with more than sixteen wellness destinations across four countries. She speaks on stress, self-care and recovery.
- **Dr Chinwe Kpaduwa**, a plastic surgeon board certified in the United States and a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons. She speaks on skin, aesthetics and ageing well, for women and men.

Chairing the panel is **Dr Debo Odulana**, a physician and Founding Partner of Consult for Africa, with sixteen years in hospital leadership across Africa, the Middle East and the UK.

Dr Odulana, over to you.

### 7:45 Questions from the room

Dr Odulana takes the questions from the stage. You run the microphone and keep the room moving.

- Ask each guest to give their first name and keep to one question.
- If a guest starts describing their own medical case, gently suggest they speak to the clinicians at After Hours.
- At 8:10, signal the chair: last two questions.

### 8:15 Vote of thanks

Ladies and gentlemen, please join me in thanking our speakers: Dr Ajelabi, Dr Adenuga, Gbemi Giwa, Joycee Awosika, Dr Kpaduwa, and our chair, Dr Odulana.

To give the vote of thanks, it is my pleasure to welcome the Group Chief Executive of Medbury Healthcare Group, the home of MedLYFE and The Lyfe Place. Please welcome Dr Itunu Akinware.

When she finishes: Thank you, Dr Akinware.

### 8:20 Close

The evening is not over. Ageless After Hours starts now: drinks, music, and the chance to talk to tonight's clinicians and speakers. Every guest goes home with an Ageless takeaway, so please collect yours on the way out.

Thank you, and enjoy the rest of the evening.

## Rules for the stage

These come from the regulators, and they apply to everything said from the stage.

- **No drug or product brand names.** Say "weight-loss medicines", not a brand.
- **No promises of results.**
- **No pitching.** Doctors may not advertise themselves, so introductions describe what people do, not what they sell.
- **If the conversation turns to who may perform which procedure,** it moves on. That is a live regulatory question in Lagos.

## Timing

- The panel has four speakers in 45 minutes, about eleven minutes each. The chair keeps time inside the panel.
- If the opening address or the panel runs over, take the time out of the questions, not out of the vote of thanks or After Hours.
- The programme must close by 8:20pm.
