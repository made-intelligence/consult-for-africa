## How to read this

Four seats are open. Dr Chinwe Kpaduwa holds the fifth, the featured fireside, so the panel needs people who cover the inside half of the proposition and one person to hold the room.

Candidates are grouped by seat and ordered by fit, not by fame. Each carries a flag where there is something you should know before anybody picks up the phone.

| Flag | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Clean | No conflict found. Approach freely. |
| Competitor | Runs or fronts a practice that competes with Medlyfe or with Lyfe Plastics. |
| CFA conflict | Already a CFA client or a live CFA prospect. Debo decides before contact. |
| Unverified | Could not confirm they are currently in the role stated. Check before approaching. |
| Handle with care | Public controversy, or a reason the room might react. |

## The rule that governs who we ask

Nobody is paid to bring us a patient, and nobody on this panel is there to sell. A moderator's honorarium is a different thing and is perfectly proper.

The two highest-value seats are the ones that look least commercial: a teaching-hospital clinician and a chair the room already respects. A panel of practice owners reads as a trade show.
