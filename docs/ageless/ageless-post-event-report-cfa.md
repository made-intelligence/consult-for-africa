## Summary

- **Event:** AGELESS took place on Wednesday 21 October at Capital Club, Lagos, hosted by MedLYFE and The Lyfe Place. Thank you for giving the vote of thanks.
- **Guests:** 58 confirmed guests, plus 17 guests they brought. 16 of the 58 are clinicians.
- **Early feedback:** the first responses rate the evening 4 and 5 out of 5, with a likelihood to recommend of 9 out of 10.
- **Follow-up:** completed on 22 October. All guests have received a thank-you email with the films, the voucher details, The Lyfe Place waiting list and a feedback form.
- **Next step:** MedLYFE converts the vouchers and booking requests into appointments.

## Recommendations for the next edition

- **Check-in:** record attendance at the door.
- **Guests brought:** collect their names and phone numbers at registration.
- **Registration:** open earlier and give each inviter a tracked link.
- **Programme:** allow more time for the panel and questions.
- **Venue and logistics:** review, as this scored lowest in early feedback.
