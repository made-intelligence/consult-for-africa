**Dr Folake Kofo-Idowu.** Double board certified physician. Founder and Medical Director of Nelia and its women's health line Nelia Oasi. Internal medicine, metabolic health and infectious diseases, working in evidence-based menopause care, hormone optimisation and preventative women's medicine.

**Dr Adedotun Ajelabi.** Clinical Lead at Medlyfe.

## Three things never to say

**Never name a prescription product.** No toxins, fillers or weight loss drugs by brand. ARCON fines start at ₦500,000 and the liability is ours.

**Never promise a result.** No before and after claims.

**Never tell anyone their place is confirmed** until they have had an invitation and used the link in it.
