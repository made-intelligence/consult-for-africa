## The evening

AGELESS is a cocktail evening at the Capital Club, Lagos, on Wednesday 21 October. Arrival half past five, programme from half past six, close at half past nine. Seventy places.

Hosted by Medlyfe. It introduces Dr Chinwe Kpaduwa and Lyfe Plastics and Dermatology to a Lagos audience that already spends on how it looks and feels.

The argument is that how you feel and how you look are one appointment, not two. A panel takes the inside. A fireside turns outward.

## Who is in the room

Women and men over forty in Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Lekki and Banana Island. People who already buy wellness, aesthetics and private healthcare and have never had the whole subject explained by people who actually know. Clinicians are welcome and a few places are kept for them.

Not a seminar crowd. They are coming for an evening out that happens to be useful.

## The four lines that sell it

**The panel.** The New Science of Ageing Well. What changes in your body, brain and skin after 40, and what you can actually do about it.

**The surgeon.** Board certified by the American Board of Plastic Surgery and a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons. Both are checkable, and in this market that claim is made more often than it is true. She is in Nigeria for a limited period.

**The room.** Seventy places. Invitations go out from the list of people who register interest, so registering is not the same as being in.

**The evening.** Cocktails, a panel, a fireside and an hour afterwards with the clinicians still in the room. Not a seminar.

## Three things never to say

**Never name a prescription product.** Not a toxin, not a filler, not a weight loss drug by brand. ARCON requires approval before exposure, the minimum fine is five hundred thousand naira, and the liability sits on us rather than on whoever said it.

**Never promise a result.** No before and after claims, no "most people see". It is an evening about understanding what changes, not a treatment being sold.

**Never say a place is confirmed** until the person has had an invitation and used the link in it. The room fills by confirmation, not by interest.
