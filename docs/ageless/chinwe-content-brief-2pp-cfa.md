## Why

Chinwe's three hundred videos already make the argument, and it is a good one. None of this day repeats it.

What they cannot do is convert a Lagos buyer, because they were made for Americans and never mention Nigeria. The woman in Ikoyi is not asking what an abdominoplasty is. She is asking who will hold the instrument, who answers at two in the morning, whether she will look obviously done, and whether she should just fly to Istanbul.

Those four questions have Lagos answers and nobody has filmed them.

Every piece is made to be sent to one person. Somebody enquires, goes quiet, and gets one video answering the exact thing she hesitated over. That is what was missing in March.

## What we need from each of you

Chinwe: the day itself, and ninety minutes **today or tomorrow morning** to cut the shot list with us. That call is now the critical path. Ten questions in the full brief only you can answer, and the question list cannot be written without them.

One is yours to refuse. The strongest idea we have is to take the real questions the March enquirers asked, anonymise them, and have you answer the commonest ten on camera. Their words, not invented ones.
