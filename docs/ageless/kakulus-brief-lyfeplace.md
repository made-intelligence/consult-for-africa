## In short

- A 90 second film that introduces LyfePlace as a destination for health, calm, movement, connection and fun.
- The direction is slow frame changes at the start, building to swift changes that lead to the climax. The copy guides the picture. The audience should be held in a trance-like state, never reading a block of text.
- Built mainly from licensed stock. Tell us in your treatment if any live footage is needed and where.
- Final file by Monday 19 October. Please reply by Wednesday 14 October with your quote.
