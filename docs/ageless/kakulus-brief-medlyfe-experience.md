## In short

- A 90 second film that shows what a guest gets at MedLYFE, and lets them see themselves in it.
- The direction is slow burn: slow changes in frame, with the copy guiding the picture. The audience should be held in a trance-like state, never reading a block of text.
- Live footage from the MedLYFE centre in Lekki, with stock where the centre cannot give the shot. The live footage comes from the half day shared with the opening film.
