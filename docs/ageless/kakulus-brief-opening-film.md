## In short

- A ten minute film on ageing that opens AGELESS at 6:30pm on Wednesday 21 October at Capital Club, Lagos. Dr Ajelabi speaks straight after it, at 6:40.
- It is built from licensed stock footage and stills under a voiceover, with live footage shot at the MedLYFE centre in Lekki cut through it.
- Its job is to put the room in the right frame: what changes as we age, and what medicine can now do about it. It informs and moves. It does not sell.
- Final file by Monday 19 October. Technical check at Capital Club on Tuesday 20 October.
- Please reply by Wednesday 14 October with whether you can deliver and your quote.

## The references

Dr Itunu Akinware chose these two films for the kind of film she wants.

| Reference | What to take from it |
| --- | --- |
| https://youtu.be/hfQo8HYmmWQ The greatest revolution in the history of medicine, Andrew Steele, Big Think | The argument: ageing is something medicine can now study, measure and act on, explained plainly, with pictures that carry the idea |
| https://youtu.be/2Ka3a3X5RRw UN Decade of Healthy Ageing, Adding Life to Years | The feeling: older people living full lives, the warmth, and the shift from more years to better years |

Treat them as tone and treatment, not a script.

## The shape of the film

A suggested structure. The voiceover script is yours to write from it, and Dr Akinware approves the script before it is recorded.

| Time | Chapter | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 1:30 | We are living longer | People everywhere, Nigerians included, are living longer than their grandparents. Many of the extra years are spent unwell. The gap between lifespan and healthspan |
| 1:30 to 4:30 | What changes | Energy, strength, sleep, weight, skin, mood and recovery. A short sequence on each, in everyday Lagos life |
| 4:30 to 6:30 | Why it happens | What science now understands about ageing, in plain language, in the manner of the first reference |
| 6:30 to 8:30 | What can be done | Measure first, then act. This is where the live footage from the centre carries the picture |
| 8:30 to 10:00 | Adding life to years | Back to people. Ends on the thought that the good years can be lengthened, and hands the room to Dr Ajelabi |

## The three materials

| Material | What we need |
| --- | --- |
| Stock footage and stills | Commercially licensed. Predominantly Black and African people, in settings that could be Lagos. No Western clinic interiors that read as foreign. Licences supplied with delivery |
| Voiceover | A Nigerian voice, warm and unhurried. Send two or three samples by Thursday 15 October so Dr Akinware can choose |
| Live footage | Half a day at the MedLYFE centre, Lekki: the diagnostics bench, an analyser, readouts on a screen, the infusion room in daylight, the team at work. Hands and backs of heads only for staff. This footage is shared with the MedLYFE Experience film, briefed separately |

## What must not be in it

- No patients. Nobody who could be read as a patient appears in any frame.
- No before and after images.
- No drug or product brand names.
- No claim of a result, a number of years gained, or a cure.
- Every statistic in the voiceover is sourced, and the source is listed with the script.
