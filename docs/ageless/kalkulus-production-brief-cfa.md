## 1. What we are asking for

A single production day in Lagos, filming Dr Chinwe Kpaduwa, a United States board certified plastic surgeon, for a library of educational video for MedLYFE Wellness and Longevity Centre.

Eight hours of recording across three locations and five lighting setups, followed by post-production into roughly fifty finished pieces. This is a talking-head and conversational shoot. There is no action, no crowd, no procedure and no patient.

We would like an itemised quotation, set out against the lines in section seven, so that pre-production, the shoot day and post-production can be considered separately.

## 5. Deliverables

| | Quantity | Specification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-form films | 6 to 8 | Four to eight minutes each. 16:9, graded, mixed, with a lower third and an end card |
| Short films | 35 to 45 | Thirty to ninety seconds each. Delivered in both 9:16 and 4:5, with burned-in captions on every version |
| B-roll | A full set | Graded, cut and delivered as selects, sufficient to cover all of the above |
| Stills | 40 or more | Retouched, delivered at full resolution and web resolution, colour and black and white |
| Masters | All of it | Every rush, in full, delivered on physical media |

Captions must be burned in rather than supplied as a sidecar file, because a large share of this will be viewed with the sound off and forwarded on WhatsApp.

Deliver the shorts named to a convention we will give you, so they can be filed and sent individually. A folder of forty files called final_v3 is of no use to the person who has to send one of them to one patient.

## 9. Conditions on set

Three things will be true on the day, and they affect the call sheet rather than the quotation. We set them out so nothing is planned that cannot be used.

**No patients, and no patient images of any kind.** Nobody who could be read as a patient appears in any frame. No before and after material will be filmed, supplied or used.

**No treatment is performed on camera, on anybody.** The treatment room setup is equipment, environment and hands only, using a colleague rather than a patient.

**No product brand names are spoken.** Our scripts already handle this. If a brand name is said in error, please flag it to the producer on the day so the line can be retaken rather than discovered in the edit.

These are regulatory requirements on our side, not stylistic ones.
