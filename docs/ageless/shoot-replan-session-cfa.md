# The content shoot

## What we agree before we book the crew

FOR
Dr Chinwe Kpaduwa and Dr Itunu Akinware

FROM
Consult for Africa

SESSION
This week, sixty minutes

---

## In short

- The shoot did not take place on 8 October, so we are planning it again with both of you before Kakulus Media is booked.
- Seven things are agreed in the session. Once they are, we confirm the crew within 24 hours and nothing else is needed from either of you until the day.
- Scripts or questions reach Dr Kpaduwa three working days before the shoot. First cuts follow ten working days after it, and final films ten working days after that.
- The films are made for one job: to be sent to one person who enquired and has not booked, answering the thing that stopped her.

## The seven things to agree

| Decision | What we agree, and who decides |
| --- | --- |
| 1. The date | A full day before Dr Kpaduwa travels. The earlier it is, the more of the films are in use while she is still here, because first cuts take ten working days. Both |
| 2. The stories | Three long and six short from the ten storyboards below, plus an optional tenth. Dr Kpaduwa |
| 3. The words | Interview, prompter or a mix. Dr Kpaduwa approves every line she says before it goes to the prompter. Dr Kpaduwa |
| 4. Medlyfe on camera | Dr Ajelabi for about two hours in the afternoon, for the conversation in story 8. Dr Akinware |
| 5. The locations | Tom Ogboi Avenue, the Kakulus studio and the Medlyfe clinic, each confirmed for the new date. Both |
| 6. Ownership and use | Who owns the footage, where it may be used, for how long, and how either of you can withdraw a piece. Both |
| 7. Sign-off | Who approves each cut, and how quickly. Both |

## Our proposal on ownership and use

This is the decision that matters most, so we suggest a starting point rather than an open question.

| | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Medlyfe owns the footage and the finished films, as the business that pays for them and takes the bookings |
| Dr Kpaduwa's image | Used only to market the Medlyfe service she leads, for 24 months from delivery. Any other use needs her written consent |
| Approval | Nothing showing Dr Kpaduwa is published until she has approved that cut |
| Withdrawal | Either of you can withdraw a piece from use with 30 days' notice, without needing to give a reason |
| Every call to action | Medlyfe's. Dr Kpaduwa explains, the clinic books |
