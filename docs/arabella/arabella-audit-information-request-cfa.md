## A note before the list

Dr Chito, thank you again for getting this started. What follows is everything we need for the audit, with the eight items that matter most at the front.

We have agreed to do the audit first and on its own. The marketing, the payer transition, the pharmacy supply work and the rest of what we discussed in the proposals all come after it, and what this finds will decide their order and their shape. So this list asks only what the audit needs. Where a question touches one of those later pieces, it is because the answer tells us something about how Arabella runs today.

You said the gap has never been strategy, it has been execution, and I agreed with you in writing. We are not here to write a view of the Abuja women's health market. We want to establish what Arabella actually earns today, where the money and the patients leak out between the first enquiry and the bank account, and whether the operation can carry the premium promise the brand is about to make.

Nothing in this list is a test. Where a record does not exist, tell us so. That absence tells us where the business runs on memory rather than on a system, which is usually more useful than a tidy file. We would rather see the real spreadsheet, the WhatsApp group where bookings get confirmed and the notebook at the front desk than anything assembled for our benefit.

I do want to look carefully at the succession from Tabitha. Arabella is a new company with its own RC number, and a lot now rests on that change at once: the HMO panels, the patients' sense of who is treating them, and whether the new company may lawfully contact the old company's patients. Section A is there to see whether one consistent set of documents supports all of that. Better we find a gap now than have an HMO medical director find it later.

Your numbers, case records, tariffs and your staff's answers stay with Consult for Africa. They are not shared with any other client of ours or with any third party unless you tell us in writing.

## How to send it

Please nominate one point of contact who can chase the rest of the team. Tolu is the obvious choice if that suits, and it helps us if requests go through one person rather than through you.

**Everything for this audit lives on one private page: consultforafrica.com/ArabellaProject.** It carries this document, an uploader that takes files straight from a phone or a laptop and tags them to the section they answer, and the four short surveys. Send things as you find them rather than waiting until you have everything, and come back to it as often as you like. The page is not listed anywhere and is not indexed. If you would rather use a shared folder in Google Drive or OneDrive, that is equally fine: make one with a sub-folder per lettered section below and give us access to it, so that nothing is lost in a thread.

Exports from a system beat typed summaries every time. A raw CSV or Excel export from the billing software, the accounting package or the appointment book is worth more to us than a neat table someone had to retype, and it carries no transcription error. Photographs of paper registers are fine and often better than nothing. Read access to a system, even for one week, is better than any export at all.

Where something is commercially or personally sensitive, mark it and send it anyway, because it is handled under the confidentiality above.

Patient records are the exception. Please do not upload anything that identifies a patient by name, phone number or address, including the patient database itself. For the analysis we need local identifiers, not names, and we will look at the full database with you on site, on your own machine. Before any identifiable patient data leaves Arabella we will put a short data processing agreement in front of you, because the Nigeria Data Protection Act makes Arabella responsible for whoever it shares patient data with.

Where a question is easier answered in conversation than in writing, write "discuss" against it and we will cover it on site.

## A. The two entities, and what the succession actually rests on

This section is short and it matters more than its length suggests. Please treat it as the priority after the eight above.

- Arabella's certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, CAC status report and RC number
- Tabitha Medical Centre's equivalent documents, and confirmation of whether that entity remains in existence, dormant or dissolved
- The relationship between the two in writing: any business transfer, asset purchase, licence or novation agreement, however informal, and if none exists, say so
- Who owns the premises, and under which entity the lease or title sits
- Which assets moved and which did not: equipment, stock, the website and domains, the social media accounts, the phone numbers, the bank accounts
- Which contracts moved: staff, suppliers, HMOs, landlord, software, the pharmacy wholesaler
- The staff position: were employees terminated and rehired, transferred, or simply continued, and what were they told in writing
- **Custody of the patient records.** Under which entity were the existing records created, under which are they now held, on what basis did they move, and what were patients told
- Any tax, PAYE, pension or regulatory liability left behind in the old entity, and any outstanding creditor
- What has already been said to HMOs, patients, staff and suppliers about the change, and in what form
- Whether the two names are being used interchangeably anywhere today: signage, invoices, receipts, prescriptions, referral letters, the ultrasound report header

## B. Ownership, governance and how decisions get made

- Current shareholding of Arabella, and whether any shares are held by a non-Nigerian person or entity
- Directors, and any shareholders' agreement
- Any other company you own or control that touches this work, including any property, equipment or supply entity
- How the business is governed today: who meets, how often, what is minuted, who signs off spending above what threshold, and who can commit the company
- Any management or board meeting notes from the last 12 months
- Bank mandate: who are the signatories, and what is the approval route for a payment
- Any loan, overdraft, equipment finance, related party balance or personal money put into the business, with terms
- Your own position: what you are paid, how it is drawn, and whether clinical income you earn elsewhere is invoiced through Arabella or personally
- Any other clinician with an economic interest, a revenue share or a sessional arrangement

## C. Licensing, regulatory standing and insurance

- Private health facility registration for the premises with the FCT health authority: the certificate, the category the premises is registered under, the expiry date, and any conditions attached
- Any inspection report or correspondence from the regulator, and any undertaking given
- The named medical director of record for the premises, and confirmation that the registration is not also being relied on by any other facility
- MDCN full registration and current annual practising licence for every doctor who works here, including visiting consultants, anaesthetists and locums
- Nursing and Midwifery Council licences for every nurse and midwife, including theatre, recovery and postnatal staff
- **The fertility service specifically:** who the responsible clinician is, who the embryologist is and what their qualification and registration are, what written guidance the unit operates to, and any accreditation or professional body affiliation held
- **The embryology laboratory and cryostorage:** the consent forms used for gametes and embryos, what patients are told about storage duration and disposal, the alarm and monitoring arrangement on the tanks, the liquid nitrogen supply and its backup, and what happens on a power failure
- Theatre and anaesthesia: who provides anaesthetic cover, on what notice, and what the arrangement is out of hours and at weekends
- **The obstetric escalation route, in writing:** the named unit you transfer to for intensive care and for neonatal intensive care, the agreement or understanding behind it, the transport arrangement, and the last three times it was used
- Blood: the transfusion arrangement, the supplying bank or service, cross matching, and how long blood takes to reach theatre in an obstetric haemorrhage
- Sterile processing: the autoclave, its validation and load records, how an instrument set is traced to a patient, and who is responsible
- Pharmacists Council premises licence and superintendent pharmacist, since medicines are dispensed on site
- Controlled drugs: the register, the storage, who holds the key, and the last reconciliation
- Medical Laboratory Science Council registration for any laboratory work done on site
- Radiation: the Nigerian Nuclear Regulatory Authority licence for any X-ray unit, plus the radiation protection officer and dosimetry records, if any such equipment is held
- Professional indemnity for you, for every other clinician, and for the facility, with sums insured and expiry dates
- Public liability, fire, burglary and equipment insurance
- Clinical and sharps waste: the contract, the carrier, and the consignment notes
- Fire safety certificate, environmental permit, and any local or signage permits
- Data protection: whether the company is registered with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission under the Act, who the data protection officer is, and what patients are told about their records
- Any current or threatened claim, regulatory complaint or litigation, and any patient complaint that escalated beyond the clinic

## D. Money: what comes in and what it costs to stand still

- Monthly management accounts or profit and loss for the last 24 months if they exist, 12 at minimum, for both entities where the period spans the change
- If formal accounts are not prepared, the income and expenditure record in whatever form it is kept, including a personal notebook
- Latest balance sheet, trial balance and chart of accounts
- Bank statements for every business account for the last 12 months, and confirmation of any personal or staff account through which business receipts have passed
- The accounting system in use, and read access or a full export
- Any accountant prepared or audited statements, and the current position on PAYE, VAT, pension remittance and company income tax for both entities
- Fixed asset register with purchase dates and values, and which entity owns each item
- **The monthly fixed cost run rate, itemised:** rent, salaries by department, diesel and power, water, internet, security, cleaning, software, insurance, waste, professional fees
- Any budget or plan for this year, and actuals against it
- The cash position today, the payables list with ages, and the worst month of the last two years with the explanation
- Cash handling: who takes money at the front desk, what the receipting process is, when it is banked, and who reconciles it against the day's activity

## E. The service ledger: volume, mix and what each episode earned

This is the most important dataset in the audit. One row per patient episode, last 24 months, across every service line. Whatever fields exist is fine. Please do not create fields that do not.

- Date, service line, procedure or package, and whether elective or emergency
- Patient age, and a local identifier so that we can trace a sample on site. Names are not needed for the analysis
- Clinician, assistant, anaesthetist and midwife where relevant
- Payer: self pay, HMO and which one, corporate, family abroad, or staff and courtesy
- **Amount quoted, amount invoiced, amount collected, and any discount or write off with the reason**
- The direct cost captured for that episode: consumables, drugs, implants, laboratory, outsourced imaging
- Length of stay, and whether discharge was as planned
- Outcome, and any return to theatre, readmission, transfer out or complication
- Separately, for maternity: bookings taken, deliveries, caesarean rate, and how many booked patients delivered elsewhere and why
- Separately, for fertility: cycles started, cycles cancelled before transfer, transfers, and clinical pregnancies, by age band
- Separately, for gynaecology surgery: procedures listed, procedures cancelled on the day, and the reason for each cancellation

## F. Pricing and the package architecture

- The current full price list, every service, and the date it was last changed
- **What a maternity package includes and, precisely, what it excludes.** What happens commercially on a caesarean, a prolonged stay, a neonatal admission, a transfer out, or a twin pregnancy
- How a package is paid: deposit, instalments, the point at which the balance is due, and what happens when a patient stops paying part way through a pregnancy
- How a surgical quote is built, who builds it, and who may vary it
- Fertility pricing: what a cycle includes, how drugs are charged, how a freeze or a subsequent transfer is priced, and what a cancelled cycle costs the patient
- Any published or advertised price, anywhere, including on the website, in a brochure, on social media or quoted in a message
- The last five quotes issued that did not convert, with whatever is known about why
- Any comparison you have already done against other Abuja facilities, formal or informal

## How the audit runs from here

So that your team knows what is coming and nobody is surprised by a request.

| Stage | What happens | What we need from the team |
|---|---|---|
| Records | Records and systems reviewed. Revenue reconstructed from the ledger and traced to the bank, and the baseline agreed with you | The eight priority items, then the rest as it is found |
| Conversations | Short conversations with staff, individually and confidentially. Nothing anyone says is attributed to them. The four surveys run alongside | Twenty minutes per person, scheduled around clinical work |
| On site | Patient journey traced end to end on live cases. Pharmacy and stock counted. The patient database reviewed with you | Access to the day as it actually runs |
| Findings | Findings tested with you before anything is written | An hour of your time |
| By the end of November | Written diagnostic: what Arabella earns, where it leaks, what the operation can carry, and what to fix in what order. Technology and records report with a prioritised roadmap | A session with you to walk through it |

*Two working practices, so that they are agreed rather than assumed. We will ask staff questions directly and confidentially, because a team tells an outsider things it will not put in front of the founder, and none of it is reported back with a name attached. And we will trace real patients through the real process rather than sampling broadly, because ten episodes followed completely tells you more than a hundred counted partially, and every gap we find that way is specific rather than theoretical.*
