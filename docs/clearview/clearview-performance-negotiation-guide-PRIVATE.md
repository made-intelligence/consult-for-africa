## THE GATE: nothing concludes until his numbers arrive

Debo, 2026-09-24: *"how can we balance this in a way that we dont conclude until
something has been received from him in terms of information."*

**The principle: price what CFA controls now, band what his numbers drive, fix the
band only when the pack arrives.**

| | Priced now | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shoot day, N3.31 m | Fixed | It is what a shoot day costs. His numbers do not change it. |
| Media and PR commission, 0% | Fixed | A position, not a calculation. |
| Conversion share / per patient fee | **Indicative** | Entirely dependent on volumes, price and repeat rates we have not seen |
| Media budget and patient targets | **Indicative** | Capped by a laboratory ceiling nobody has measured |

**Three stages, slides 12 to 14.** Stage one he sends ten numbers and runs the
surveys, at no fee and no contract either side. Stage two CFA prices it off his
numbers and holds the price thirty days. Stage three is the first shoot and the
campaign. **Nobody is committed until three.**

**The ten numbers are the ask, not the 123 question discovery form.** A short list
comes back; a long form does not. The questionnaire stays as the deeper instrument
for when he is engaged.

**Why this protects CFA specifically.** Under a performance structure a wrong
assumption costs us, not him. We assumed 15 cycles a month at N4.75m, 7% enquiry to
cycle, and a laboratory ceiling of 30 that we invented. **If any of those are
materially wrong we have priced our own risk incorrectly and he has lost nothing.**
The gate is the only thing standing between us and that.

It is also the strongest sales position in the deck, and it should be delivered as
confidence rather than caution: *any agency will put a price in front of you this
week; we will not, because we are the ones carrying the risk, so we are the ones who
need the numbers.*

**It also defers the open structural question.** Whether the fee is 20% of collected
revenue or a fixed N1.0m per patient who starts treatment does not have to be settled
until stage two, and his numbers are what should settle it. Do not raise the choice
with him; present the mechanism and price it once.

## The shape

**Two packed shoot days a year, at the C4A office.** Everybody in on the same day:
the doctor, consented patients, the team, referring colleagues. Debo, 2026-09-24:
*"better 2 shoots per year", "so we get as many testimonials as possible", "we also
get as many scripted content as possible."*

Each day yields a deep enough library to feed **two three month campaigns**, so two
days covers twelve months of continuous advertising. Targets per day: 8 to 12 patient
testimonials, 20 to 30 scripted pieces, 60 to 80 edited assets.

**Media is two channels, not one.** Paid advertising plus **PR placed on Nigerian
blogs** (*"PR on blogs"*), 5 placements a campaign, written out of the same shoot day.
The PR argument is that she searches Clearview before she calls, and a placed article
keeps ranking long after an advert stops.

**The attribution window is 24 months per patient and is NOT the campaign length.**
The campaign runs three months; the lag from enquiry to first cycle is about four
months, and her second cycle, storage and delivery come much later. **If he tries to
match attribution to campaign length, refuse it. That is the deal collapsing, not a
discount.**

### Why the instrument changed, 2026-09-24

Debo: *"20% is high."* It was, and the honest reason is not the headline: **20 per
cent of GROSS revenue was about 45 per cent of his CONTRIBUTION** on those patients,
so on profit it read as an even split with the man who owns the laboratory, the staff
and all the clinical risk.

So the instrument changed rather than the number being shaved:

| | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | 20% of 24 months of collected revenue | **N0.95m flat, per patient who starts treatment** |
| Share of his contribution | ~45% | **~26%** |
| CFA, year one base | N184m | **N110m** |
| He keeps | N690m | **N765m** |
| Cash lands | months 6 to 30 | **months 5 to 10** |
| Requires auditing his books | Yes, monthly, for 2 years | **No** |

**Express it as one fifth of a single cycle, not as a naira figure alone**, so it
re-prices automatically if his cycle price is higher than the N4.75m assumed. Fix the
multiple at stage two off his real price.

**What CFA gave up**: the tail. Her second cycle, her frozen transfer, her storage and
her delivery are now entirely his, worth about N3.24m of the N7.99m she is worth. That
is the concession that buys the whole deal, and **slide 7 sells it hard** rather than
hiding it: we are paid on the hardest part, getting her through the door the first
time, and everything after is yours.

**What CFA gained**: no book audit, cash three times faster, no year-two argument
about who her second cycle belonged to, and a number he can hold in his head. The
attribution slide collapsed from a revenue audit to a single question, which is worth
more than the money.

## Attribution is the whole fight, not the percentage

He will argue that patients would have come anyway. The defences, in order:

1. **Only first contact counts.** Anyone already in his records is excluded, and the
   deck says so. Concede this loudly and early, it costs nothing and buys trust.
2. **Tag at source from day one.** The enquiry register from the earlier scope is now
   load-bearing infrastructure, not a nice-to-have. **If he will not run it, there
   is no deal**, because there is no way to count what we are owed.
3. **Ambiguous cases go to him.** Written into the deck. Cheap, and it removes the
   argument that we mark our own homework.
4. **Audited by anyone he likes, at our cost.**

## Real risks, ours not his

- **Content dependency.** If he does not sit for the shoot, nothing exists to run.
  Two hours a month sounds trivial and is the single most common point of failure
  in this model. Get the shoot dates in the contract, not the plan.
- **Agency capacity.** Nimi and Ejiro already run the agency and the four brands,
  per [[project_marketing_handbook]]. **Confirm they can carry a fertility client
  with a monthly shoot before signing anything.** This is the constraint most likely
  to bite and it is entirely ours.
- **Lab ceiling.** Slide 7. Past about N5.3 m a month of media he runs out of
  laboratory, and we stop earning on volume we generated. The upsell at that point
  is a second embryologist, not a bigger budget.
- **He may already have an agency.** Ask before sending. A displaced incumbent is a
  political problem we should know about first.
- **Assumptions are unmeasured.** Cost per qualified enquiry, enquiry to cycle, and
  the repeat and capture rates are all guesses. Under this model a wrong assumption
  costs CFA the upside, not him. The first ninety days exist to replace them, and
  the deck says so.

## Pushback

**"20% is a lot."** It is a lot of something that does not exist yet. You pay it out
of revenue from patients you did not have, after you have already collected the
money. Compare it to an agency retainer plus 18 per cent of your media, payable
whether or not anyone walks in.

**"Why am I paying for content as well?"** Because the team is real, it is salaried,
and it works whether or not the campaigns land. Slide 5 shows you every line of it at
cost plus 25 per cent, and there is no setup fee. We are already carrying the media
commission and the conversion risk. If we carried production too we would simply be
your employee, paid late.

**"Can you take it all on conversion?"** Only at a much higher share, and it would
make us reckless about who we bring you. Say that plainly, because it is true.

**"What if they would have found me anyway?"** Then they would not be attributed.
Only first contact through a campaign we ran counts, and if the source is unclear it
does not count.

## The Mezo channel (added 2026-10-11)

Slide 9 and a fifth line on the deal slide. Mezo builds the organic side at its own
cost: search pages for the whole journey, the Couple Fertility Check, `/ivf-from-abroad`
in GBP and USD, and network scans for remote monitoring. Plan:
`~/Downloads/Mezo_v2/growth/seo/IVF_CAPTURE.md`.

**One fee, either channel.** N0.95m per patient who starts treatment, first contact only.
Do not create a second rate for Mezo. Two rates invite an argument about which channel she
came through.

**His price, no markup.** Unlike House of Refuge (+10%), Mezo publishes his cycle price as
his. He also sells direct off the C4A campaigns, so two prices for one cycle would kill
trust in both. Mezo earns the fee on the cycle and the network spread on the Fertility
Check, which Mezo bills. If he asks what Mezo makes on the Check, say so plainly.

**The attribution argument goes away on this channel.** She pays on Mezo, so her source is
on the receipt. Use it as proof that we mean what slide 14 says about the register.

**Ask for the search approvals separately, now.** Name, price sheet, reviewing consultant
(with MDCN number) and street address. Search takes three to six months to rank, so it
should not wait for stage two. It costs him nothing and starts the clock.

**Do not model organic.** The deck excludes it on purpose. If he asks, the private band is
1 to 4 patients a month after month 3 to 6, unmeasured. Do not say it out loud as a
forecast.

**Do not publish his "40 to 60 per cent" success claim** on Mezo pages. Ask for live birth
rate by age band (number 4 on the gate). Only that gets published.

**Mainland and Abuja monitoring:** Beacon Ikeja and Sangotedo are real today. Abuja is
"next", not live: Key Imaging Utako is still in the pipeline. Do not promise Abuja dates.
