## What changed after our meeting

This schedule replaces the one issued on 23 September. At that meeting you clarified that the majority of clinical risk management is already in place, and that what the Hospital wants is an enterprise grade framework covering operational, financial, cultural and reputational risk, with clinical integrated into it rather than rebuilt.

That is a materially different engagement. The deep clinical diagnostic, the case level tracing and the patient safety build all come out, and project coordination moves to your own team. **The fee falls by 41 per cent as a result, from N35,694,000 to N21,060,000.** Our rates are unchanged and the term stays at the six months your terms of reference set. What changed is the amount of work the engagement actually needs.

## How this fee is built

This schedule accompanies our proposal of 30 March 2026, revised after our meeting. It prices the engagement **by phase, against defined deliverables**, so you can see what each stage of the work costs and what it produces. If a phase is deprioritised it comes straight off the total.

The core system is designed, built and activated in the first twelve weeks. The remaining months are embedding and independent assurance against the baseline we establish in week two, within the six month period your terms of reference set.

| Phase | Period | What it produces | Fee |

The programme rate applies because these five phases are one continuous mandate rather than five separate engagements, so mobilisation, familiarisation and reporting are carried once instead of five times.

## Payment

Payment is tied to delivery milestones, weighted to mobilisation because the diagnostic and framework phases carry the bulk of the senior effort and are delivered early.

| Milestone | Share | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement confirmation and mobilisation | 60% | N12,636,000 |
| Approval of the Risk Management Framework and policy suite, close of Phase 2 | 20% | N4,212,000 |
| Completion of implementation, training and handover, close of Phase 4 | 20% | N4,212,000 |

The Phase 5 assurance period is included in the fee above and carries no further payment.

## What is included

- All deliverables listed in your terms of reference and in our proposal, including the diagnostic report, the risk management framework and policy suite, the risk register, the key risk indicator framework, the risk dashboard, training materials and session reports, and the final report and handover.
- The anonymous staff safety culture instrument and the patient and caregiver experience instrument, deployed as live forms with printed materials, and the analysis of both.
- All training delivery, materials and competency assessments.
- Coaching and certification of your internal Clinical Governance Champion.
- Attendance at the Board Audit and Risk Sub-committee for the duration of the engagement.
- Training materials licensed to Duchess International Hospital in perpetuity.

## Taxes

Your submission requirements ask for taxes to be stated, so they are set out in full rather than left as a footnote.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fee payable | N21,060,000 |
| Value added tax at 7.5 per cent | N1,579,500 |
| **Total invoice value** | **N22,639,500** |

Withholding tax on professional services, currently 5 per cent, is deducted at source from the fee element and remitted to the Federal Inland Revenue Service on our behalf. It is a deduction from our fee rather than an additional cost to the Hospital, and we will require the credit note in the normal way.

## What is not included

- Any external accreditation body fee, survey fee or application cost.
- Software or system licences purchased in the hospital's name.
- Travel outside Lagos, reimbursed at cost with no mark up.

## Validity

This schedule is valid for sixty days from the date above. Fees are capped at the total stated and billed against the milestone schedule.
