# Where the Culture Programme Stands
**A snapshot for the founders, three weeks in**
Prepared for: The founders of Haven Paediatric Centre
Prepared by: Dr Debo Odulana, Founding Partner
Date: 24 October 2026
Circulation: Haven founders and Consult for Africa

---

## 1. The position in one page

::: stats
16 of 22 | staff signed in | up from 2 on 9 October
45 | tasks completed this week | against 11 in the first fortnight
9 | people completing tasks | across nursing, medical, pharmacy, lab and admin
:::

The team has started using the staff page, and the pace is rising week on week. Three weeks after the town hall, most of the hospital has signed in at least once and twelve people used it this week.

Use is still concentrated. Nursing carries most of the activity, the morning huddle has not yet been recorded, and six members of staff have not signed in. Dr Tanwa and Abigail are sitting with each of them on shift next week.

---

## 2. What is in place

| | Status |
|---|---|
| Staff page and sign-in | Live. Sign-in now works by email or mobile number |
| Daily and weekly tasks | 17 tasks across every department, each one tickable on a phone |
| Reminders | The system follows up anything not recorded, first as a nudge, then a flag to the line manager |
| Clinical standards | 33 standards drafted, waiting for clinical approval |
| Speaking up | Near misses, time wasted and recognition now ask for one sentence, so each one can be acted on and closed out with the team |
