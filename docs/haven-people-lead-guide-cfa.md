## 2. Read this first. The sensitivities

### The incident, and how we hold it

In May 2026 Haven lost a child who was referred in already critically ill. The loss is not attributable to the facility, and it is what prompted the leadership to call us.

We do not raise it, in writing or in conversation, ever. If a member of staff raises it with you, listen with care, do not ask questions about it, and move the conversation on gently. Never write anything that connects it to a role, a gap in staffing or a person. If you find yourself about to, stop and call me.

### Pay, and the adjustment round

Earlier this year Haven ran a salary adjustment round that staff read as unfair, because some received much larger increases than others and nobody explained why. It is still a live grievance. Expect it to come up in your conversations.

You do not comment on it, defend it or criticise it. Write down what the person says in their own words, thank them, and tell them it will go to the people who can answer it. Never discuss one person's pay with another, never confirm what somebody else earns, and never send salary figures by WhatsApp or in a group email. Payroll figures come from Haven's Head of Finance when a contract needs them, and they go nowhere else.

### Nobody's job is ours to promise or threaten

You will be asked, directly, whether somebody's job is safe. The honest answer is that this work is about writing down properly what each person does and making sure the hospital has the right posts for the work in front of it. Decisions about posts belong to Haven's founders. Do not promise anybody their job is safe and do not hint that it is not. If anybody presses, tell them you will make sure the question reaches the right person, and bring it to me.

### Personal data

Contracts, ID copies, licences, bank details and pay are personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and health information is sensitive data with stricter rules. Before you scan or upload the first signed document, ask me where the files are to be kept. Not on WhatsApp, not in email threads, not in a personal Google Drive and not on your own laptop.

### The relationship

I was invited to Haven's board from inception but do not sit on it. Dr Shakirah Saliu is a former co-founder of mine. Know it, never imply leverage from it, and never discuss the commercial terms of our engagement with anybody at Haven.

## 5. Staff realignment

Realignment means making the posts on paper match the work the hospital actually needs, and putting each person in the post that matches what they do. It is not a restructuring, and you should not use the word realignment with staff. To them it is "making sure everyone's role is written down properly".

### Establishment is not headcount

Establishment is the number of posts the hospital needs to run safely, by role and by shift. Headcount is the number of people employed. Haven has been running with fewer posts than its service needs, and the gap has been filled by people stretching across roles. Dr Tanwa works out the establishment from the beds, the shifts and the volume. You work out who is doing what today.

### How to do it

Start from three lists: the organogram, the payroll and the last two months of rosters. Then sit with each person for fifteen minutes on shift, ideally with their department head's knowledge, and ask them to walk you through a normal day and a bad day. Write what they tell you in their words.

For every person, fill in one row of the realignment table.

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Name | As on the payroll |
| Title on paper | From the payroll or offer letter |
| What they actually do | From the conversation and the roster |
| Hours and shifts actually worked | From the roster |
| Reports to, in practice | Who they actually go to, which is often not who the organogram says |
| Registration held | NMCN, MDCN, MLSCN or PCN, with the expiry date |
| Proposed post | Left blank until Dr Tanwa has drafted the establishment |
| Note | Anything that needs a decision. Factual, never a judgement about the person |

When the table is complete, every row falls into one of four groups: the person and the post match; the person is doing work that belongs to a different post; the person is covering two posts; or there is a post with nobody in it. The last group is the vacancy list for the founders. The middle two are where the conversations in section 9 matter most.

### What you never do

Never tell a person their proposed post before the founders have approved the table. Never put a judgement about performance in the table: it describes posts and work, not how well anybody does it. If you find somebody working outside the scope of their registration, take it to Dr Tanwa that day, privately. It is a clinical safety matter for her and Dr Odedina to resolve through the roster, and the person concerned should only ever hear about it as a change to their shifts.

## 6. Job descriptions

One page per post, not per person. Five registered nurses share one Registered Nurse job description.

### How to write them

With the people who do the job, never at them. Use what you heard in the realignment conversations, draft it, then sit with the department head and one person in the post and read it through together. Most of the time the existing practice is sound and nobody has written it down. Your draft is a record, not a reform. Where something in the draft surprises them, you have usually found a constraint nobody told you about, so write that down too.

Every job description has the same nine parts, in this order: post title and department; reports to; who reports to this post; the purpose of the post in one sentence; six to eight main responsibilities, each starting with a verb; qualifications, registration and experience required; the working pattern and shifts; how performance is reviewed; and the date, version number and who approved it.

Keep the responsibilities to the work the post does every week. A list of twenty duties tells nobody anything, and "any other duties as assigned" belongs once, at the end, not throughout.

The registration line matters more in a hospital than anywhere else. Nurses are registered with the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria, doctors with the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria, laboratory scientists with the Medical Laboratory Science Council of Nigeria, and pharmacists and pharmacy technicians with the Pharmacists Council of Nigeria. Each job description says which registration the post requires and that it must be kept current. The job description is where the clinical scope of a post is fixed in writing, which is why Dr Odedina signs every clinical one.

### Approval

Clinical job descriptions go to Dr Odedina through Dr Tanwa. Non-clinical ones go to Dr Tanwa. All of them then go to the founders as one set alongside the establishment. Nothing reaches a member of staff until it is approved, dated and numbered. An undated job description is a rumour.

The worked example in section 10 is the one to copy.

## 7. New contracts

### Why this needs care

Nigerian employment law requires an employer to give every worker written terms of employment within three months of starting. Most of Haven's team either have no written contract or have an offer letter that does not cover what a contract should. Putting that right protects them and protects Haven, and it is one of the clearest signals the founders can send that they take their staff seriously.

It also carries more legal weight than anything else in this workstream. So the template is drafted once, reviewed once by a Nigerian employment lawyer that Haven appoints, approved by the founders, and then used for everybody. You do not draft a contract from scratch for any individual, and you never change a clause for one person without my written agreement.

### The rules that protect staff

A new contract must not make anybody worse off than they are today on pay, hours, leave or any benefit they already have, unless they agree to it in writing after it has been explained to them. If any draft would do that, stop and bring it to me.

The start date in the contract is the date the person first joined Haven, not the date they sign. A new contract must never reset someone's length of service, because length of service is what notice periods and many entitlements are counted from.

Probation applies to new joiners only. Somebody who has worked at Haven for a year is not put on probation by a new piece of paper.

### Pension and benefits

The Pension Reform Act 2014 makes a contributory pension compulsory for an employer of Haven's size, with a minimum employer contribution of ten per cent. That makes pension a legal question as well as the staff's loudest request, and the contract has to state the position. Health cover is not compulsory in the same way, but the contract states whatever the founders decide. Neither is yours to answer. I will take both to the founders, and the contracts wait for their decision rather than going out silent on it.

### Issuing a contract

Each contract is handed over in person, one to one, with the job description attached. You explain what has changed and what has not. The person takes it away for at least three working days, can ask any question, and can ask a family member or adviser to read it. Then they sign two copies, a founder countersigns, the person keeps one and the other goes into their file.

Record each step in the tracker: drafted, approved, explained, signed by the person, countersigned, filed. A contract that has been signed but not filed does not exist when somebody needs it.

## 8. Objectives

The audit's six-month test is simple. Every member of staff can state their five objectives and has had at least two documented conversations about them. At day sixty I will ask three people at random what their objectives are.

### Departments first, then people

Department objectives come first, written by the department head with Dr Tanwa and with your help on the wording. Personal objectives are then drawn from the department's, so that each person can see how their work adds up to their team's.

### What a good objective looks like

Five or fewer. Each one names a measure, a number, where the number comes from and when it is checked. Each one is something the person can actually affect. Each one ties to something in the growth plan or the culture plan, so it is clear why it matters. If a nurse cannot explain an objective to a colleague in one sentence, it is not finished.

Where the data does not exist yet, the first objective is to start recording it. That is a perfectly good objective for the first quarter.

### The line you must not cross

Never set an objective for a doctor or nurse based on the number of admissions, length of stay or tests ordered. Clinical decisions have to be kept separate from targets, because an objective that rewards volume puts patients at risk, whatever the intention. Clinical objectives are about quality and reliability: documentation complete at the point of care, standards followed, checks done, incidents reported, resuscitation training current. Dr Odedina signs every one of them.

Objectives are not pay yet. Do not tell anybody that money attaches to them. Whether and how the founders link reward to performance is a later decision, and a promise made early that is not kept would undo everything the objectives are for.

### The conversations that follow

An objective nobody talks about is a document in a drawer. Each person gets a fifteen-minute monthly one to one with their department head and a quarterly review. Your job is to schedule them, make sure a two-line note of each is kept, and chase the ones that slip, which is the same chase you already run for the culture plan.

## 9. Talking to staff

Every piece of this work starts with a conversation, and how the conversation goes decides how the paperwork is received.

::: script
"I'm Abigail, I work with Dr Tanwa. We're making sure everybody's role at Haven is written down properly, so everyone knows what is expected of them and what they can expect back. Can you walk me through a normal shift for you? And then a hard one?"
:::

Open the same way with everybody, so nobody feels singled out. Talk to people on shift, at a moment their department head has agreed, and never pull a nurse away from a patient to do it.

Listen more than you talk. Write down what they say in their words, not your summary. Tell them who will see your notes: Dr Tanwa and me, and nobody else at Haven in a form that identifies them.

If somebody is anxious about their job, say what is true: the work is about writing roles down properly, and decisions about posts sit with the founders. Then bring their concern to me so it is not lost.

If somebody raises a grievance about pay, the adjustment round, a colleague or a manager, thank them, write it down, and tell them it will reach somebody who can answer it. Do not agree, disagree, or promise an outcome.

If somebody raises the May loss, follow section 2.

When a conversation goes badly, stop, thank the person, and tell Dr Tanwa the same day. There will always be another day to finish.

::: break

## 10. Examples to copy

### A finished job description: Registered Nurse

::: glance
Post | Registered Nurse
Department | Nursing
Reports to | Senior Registered Nurse
Reports to this post | None
Version | Draft 1, for review by Dr Odedina
:::

**Purpose of the post.** To deliver safe, documented nursing care to children and newborns on the ward, in the neonatal unit and in outpatient triage, on the shifts set by the roster.

**Main responsibilities**

- Assess, plan and deliver nursing care to the patients allocated on each shift, within the standards and protocols approved by the Medical Director.
- Give medicines as prescribed, and record each dose on the drug chart at the time it is given.
- Hand over every patient at each shift change using the agreed handover format, and record that the handover took place.
- Complete the crash trolley check at the start of every shift and sign the checklist.
- Recognise a deteriorating patient and escalate to the medical officer on duty without delay, following the escalation pathway.
- Report every incident and near miss through the hospital's reporting form on the same shift.
- Keep the nursing record complete, legible and signed at the point of care.
- Support parents with clear information and refer any question outside nursing scope to the medical team.

**Qualifications and registration.** Registered Nurse with current registration with the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria, kept current throughout employment. Current basic life support certification, with paediatric and neonatal resuscitation training completed within six months of joining. Paediatric or neonatal experience preferred.

**Working pattern.** Shifts as set by the published roster, including nights, weekends and public holidays. Swaps agreed in advance with the Senior Registered Nurse and recorded.

**How performance is reviewed.** Against the individual objectives agreed each year, in a monthly one to one with the Senior Registered Nurse and a quarterly review.

**Approved by.** Dr Odedina, Medical Director. Date. Version.

### Department objectives: Nursing, first quarter

| Objective | Measure | Where the number comes from | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every handover recorded | Share of shift changes with a completed handover record. Target: all of them | The handover log | Weekly by the Senior Registered Nurse |
| The crash trolley ready every shift | Share of shifts with a signed trolley check. Target: all of them | The trolley checklist | Weekly |
| Drug charts complete | Share of sampled charts with every dose signed at the time. Target: 95 per cent | Ten charts sampled each month with Dr Odedina | Monthly |
| Speaking up is normal | Incident and near miss reports from nursing each month. Target: rising, and every one closed out | The incident register | Monthly |
| Resuscitation training current | Share of nurses with current basic life support. Target: all of them by March | The training register | Monthly |

The fourth objective is the one people misread. A rise in reports is good news: it means people feel safe to speak up. Say so whenever you share the numbers.

### Contract checklist

Every contract issued from the approved template must have each of these filled in and checked before it goes to a founder for signature.

- [ ] Haven's full registered name and address as the employer
- [ ] The person's full legal name, as on their ID
- [ ] Post title, exactly matching the approved job description, which is attached
- [ ] Original start date at Haven, not the date of signing
- [ ] Probation stated only for joiners in the last six months, and not otherwise
- [ ] Pay as confirmed in writing by the Head of Finance, unchanged unless the founders approved a change in writing
- [ ] Hours and shift pattern, matching the job description
- [ ] Annual leave, sick leave and public holidays
- [ ] Pension and any benefits, exactly as the founders decided
- [ ] Notice period on each side
- [ ] Confidentiality and patient data
- [ ] Reference to the staff handbook, code of conduct, disciplinary and grievance procedures
- [ ] Registration requirement, for clinical posts
- [ ] Nothing in the contract leaves the person worse off than today without their written agreement

### Personnel file checklist

One file per person, kept where I confirm, holding:

- [ ] Signed contract, countersigned
- [ ] Approved job description, the version they were given
- [ ] Copy of ID
- [ ] Copies of qualification certificates
- [ ] Current practising licence, with the expiry date recorded in the tracker
- [ ] Resuscitation and other training certificates
- [ ] Next of kin and emergency contact
- [ ] Pension fund administrator details, once the pension position is settled
- [ ] Signed acknowledgement of each protocol that applies to the post
- [ ] Objectives for the year, and the notes of each review

### The tracker

One row per person, one column per step: realignment conversation held; proposed post approved; job description approved; contract drafted; contract explained; contract signed; contract countersigned; file complete; licence expiry; department objectives agreed; personal objectives agreed; last one to one. Dr Tanwa and I should be able to open it at any moment and see exactly where everybody is.

## 11. How we work

Send Dr Tanwa and me a note every Friday, copying Ezinne, kept to a page: what moved this week, what is stuck and why, and what you need decided. Every draft goes to Dr Tanwa first. Anything going to the founders comes through me.

You will get a written note back on every piece of work you send, saying what was right and what to change. If a week goes by without one, chase me for it.

And tell me where the plan is wrong. You and Dr Tanwa will be in the building talking to twenty people, and I will not. If the order, the timetable or any example in this guide does not fit what you are seeing, say so, say why, and bring what you would do instead.
