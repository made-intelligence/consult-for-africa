# MEZO NETWORK: ASSESSOR'S FIELD GUIDE

Internal. For the person doing the visit, with the score sheet open on a tablet or printed.

## THE RULES

1. **Seen, not said.** An item is met only on evidence you saw: a document, a log, a record, the thing itself working. "We do that" scores 0 until you see it.
2. **Score what is normal, not what is best.** A log kept for the last week but not the last three months scores 1.
3. **The assessor has no interest in the facility.** If you have one, stop and say so. A surgeon-owned facility is assessed by someone else.
4. **Write the evidence in the sheet as you go,** in a few words: "autoclave log Jul to Oct, BI weekly", "no capnograph in theatre 2".
5. **Photograph equipment and documents, never patients or identifiable records.**

## PART A: WHAT COUNTS AS EVIDENCE

| Ref | Met when you have seen |
|---|---|
| A1 | The certificate, in date, naming the services in use |
| A2 | The licence for the current year, and that this doctor is medical director of record here |
| A3 | PCN and MLSCN certificates, or the signed arrangement with registered providers |
| A4 | The NNRA licence, if X-ray or a C-arm is on site. Not applicable otherwise, scored Yes |
| A5 | The policy schedule, and indemnity certificates for the theatre clinicians |
| A6 | Completed checklists in all five traced records, signed at all three stages |
| A7 | Pulse oximeter, BP and ECG at every theatre and recovery bed, working, and a capnograph wherever airways are used |
| A8 | The rota and the anaesthetic charts of the traced cases show a named anaesthesia provider throughout |
| A9 | The trolley, a working defibrillator (switch it on), and a daily check sheet signed for the last month |
| A10 | Oxygen at each point, the reserve cylinders and their log, working suction |
| A11 | The generator and changeover, and a battery backup for theatre monitors and lights. Ask when power last failed in theatre |
| A12 | The autoclave, its cycle log, chemical indicators every load, biological indicator at least weekly |
| A13 | The waste contract and colour-coded segregation in theatre and ward |
| A14 | The written plan, and the transfer agreement with the named hospital and ambulance |
| A15 | The blood arrangement in writing, and how long it actually takes |
| A16 | Operative and anaesthetic notes and a consent form recording the risks in each traced record |
| A17 | The named person, and where patient data is kept and who can see it |
| A18 | The fire certificate in date, clear exits, extinguishers in date |

## PART B: THE FIVE-PATIENT TRACE

Choose five surgical patients from the last three months yourself from the theatre register, including the most recent major case and one that had a complication if there was one. For each, find:

- consent form with the risks discussed;
- pre-anaesthetic assessment;
- WHO checklist, all three stages;
- antibiotic given, and the time against the incision;
- anaesthetic chart and recovery chart;
- operative note;
- implant stickers, if an implant was used;
- the sterilisation load record for the instrument set;
- observations and early warning scores on the ward;
- venous thromboembolism assessment;
- discharge summary, and whether it went to the referring doctor.

Each missing link is a finding against B3, B4, B6 or B5. Record how many of the five had each link.

## PART C: QUESTIONS THAT FIND THE REAL ANSWER

- "Show me the last time a patient deteriorated after surgery. What happened, and who decided?" (B5, A14)
- "If a batch of implants were recalled tomorrow, how would you find the patients?" (B6, Tier 3)
- "When did the power last go off during an operation?" (A11)
- "Who checks the trolley, and what happens if they are off?" (A9, B7)
- "How do you know your infection rate?" (B4, Tier 3)
- "Who covers the ward at 2am on a Sunday?" (B5, B7, Tier 2)

## CLOSING THE VISIT

Tell the medical director what is missing and that they have five working days to send it. Do not give a Tier on the day. Score within two working days, have a second reviewer check any minimum marked No and any Tier 3 decision, and send the report within ten working days.
