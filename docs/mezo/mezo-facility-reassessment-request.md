# MEZO NETWORK: FACILITY ASSESSMENT VISIT

**To:** ___________________________, Medical Director, ___________________________

**From:** Mezo Surgery

**Visit date:** ______________ 2026, from 9:00am, about four hours

## WHY WE ARE COMING

Every facility that hosts Mezo Surgery patients is assessed against one published standard, the Mezo Network Facility Assessment Criteria, which is attached. The assessment decides whether the facility joins the network and which class of surgery it may host: day case, inpatient, or major and implant surgery. The Mezo Surgery Facility Agreement is signed after it, for the class the facility is approved for.

You receive the full score sheet afterwards, with the evidence behind any item that falls short, and a written report within ten working days.

## PLEASE HAVE READY BEFORE THE VISIT

Copies can be emailed in advance or kept in one folder for the day.

1. Registration certificate from the state regulator for private health establishments, for the services you provide.
2. The medical director's current MDCN practising licence.
3. Pharmacy and laboratory registration (PCN and MLSCN), or the written arrangement with registered providers.
4. NNRA licence, if you use X-ray or a C-arm.
5. Public liability insurance, and the indemnity certificates of the clinicians who work in theatre.
6. Current fire safety certificate.
7. Your written arrangement for intensive care transfer (if intensive care is not on site), and for blood.
8. A list of theatres, recovery beds, ward beds, and any high dependency or intensive care beds.
9. The equipment list for theatre and recovery, with the latest maintenance and calibration records.
10. Autoclave cycle logs and indicator test results for the last three months.
11. The clinical waste collection contract.
12. A staff list with licence numbers, and the theatre and ward rotas for the last month.
13. Theatre lists for the last three months.
14. The procedures you would like approval for.

## HOW THE DAY RUNS

| Time | What happens | Who we need |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | Opening, and a check of the documents above | Medical director |
| 9:45 | Walk round: theatre, sterilisation, recovery, ward, pharmacy, laboratory, power and oxygen | Theatre lead, nurse in charge |
| 11:15 | Five recent surgical patients traced through their records, from consent to discharge | Records officer |
| 12:15 | Short conversations with theatre, recovery and ward staff | Two or three staff on duty |
| 12:45 | First impressions and anything still to send | Medical director |

Nothing about patient care should change for the visit. We do not need to observe an operation. We will look at records with the medical director or a named member of staff, and we will not take copies of patient records away.

## AFTERWARDS

Within ten working days: the report, the score sheet and the decision. If anything is missing on the day, you will have five working days to send it before we score it.
