## Part One: Your role

### 1.1 What an analyst does here

- Record: keep each engagement's notes, actions and documents current and filed against the right milestone.
- Meetings: prepare the pack before a client meeting and write the minutes and actions after it, the same day.
- Chasing: follow up client inputs the engagement lead is waiting on, and log every chase on the platform.
- Analysis: first-draft research, data cleaning and tables for the engagement lead to review.
- Office: one standing weekly item each (Part Four), plus tasks Ezinne assigns.

### 1.2 The reporting line

| Person | Role | What goes to them |
|---|---|---|
| Ezinne Orji | Executive Assistant to the Founding Partner, Abuja | Your tasks, reviews, blocks, evaluation, leave |
| The engagement lead on each project | Delivery | Questions about the content of the work |
| Abigail Oladejo | Administrative Assistant, reports to Debo | How things are done day to day |
| Debo | Founding Partner | Nothing directly unless Ezinne routes it |

Ezinne and the engagement leads agree your priorities between them. If two people give you work that clashes, tell Ezinne and let her decide. Do not try to resolve it yourself by working late.

### 1.3 The rules of the task system

- Every task has a brief (why it matters) and a definition of done (what finished looks like). If either is missing or unclear, ask before starting.
- The person who assigned a task reviews it. Submit to them, not to someone else.
- Move a task to In progress when you start. Ezinne reads the board, not your messages.
- Blocked: mark it blocked and write the reason in one or two sentences. It goes straight to the assigner.
- Changes requested: work comes back with a written note. Read the note, fix what it says, and keep the note for next time.
- Check-in date: most tasks carry one. Have something to show on that day, even if it is unfinished.
- Record your actual time when you submit. If it was triple the estimate, say so; that teaches the person who estimated it.

### 1.4 What your account reaches

- Yours: My desk, Tasks, Pipeline, Communications (prepare, not send), Campaigns (prepare, not send), Commitments, Decisions, Meetings, Rhythm, Knowledge, Asset Library.
- Client work: Projects, Deliverables, Time and Tools appear in your sidebar because you are staffed on engagements.
- Not yours: Finance, rates, user administration and the client portals. Nothing leaves the firm under your name until Ezinne has approved it.

## Part Five: How you are evaluated

Ezinne evaluates you on the 28th of each month on the platform. The conversation happens before the form is filled in, so nothing in it should surprise you.

| Area | What Ezinne looks at |
|---|---|
| Technical skills | Accuracy of records, quality of first drafts, how much rework your work needs |
| Communication | Clear written updates, minutes the client could read, blocks raised early |
| Professionalism | Deadlines met or renegotiated before they pass, confidentiality, punctuality |
| Initiative | Spotting what is missing and fixing it, without being asked |
| Teamwork | Helping each other and Abigail, handing over cleanly |

- Scale: 1 to 5. A 3 means doing the job well. A 5 is rare and means operating above your level.
- Written feedback: at least one specific strength and one specific area to develop each month.
- Promotion: from month three, Ezinne can recommend you for the next consultant tier. That is earned on evidence in the records, not on time served.
- Your part: before the 28th, send Ezinne three lines. What you delivered, what went wrong, what you want to learn next month.

## Part Six: How we write, and what never leaves the building

- Answer first. The first line says the result or the ask.
- Short. Cut every sentence that is not information.
- No dashes as punctuation in anything a client sees. Use a comma or a full stop.
- Names: Dr and surname for doctors until they tell you otherwise.
- Confidentiality: no client names, findings or figures outside the firm, including on personal social media and in conversation. In a market this small, a few harmless details identify a hospital.
- Money: you will see no fees. If a client raises money with you, say the engagement lead will come back to them, and tell Ezinne the same day.
- Sending: nothing goes to a client in your name until Ezinne has approved it.
