# The Development Plan

**Office of the Founding Partner**

Prepared for Abigail Oladejo, Favour Aina and Ruth Oladejo
Consult for Africa internal. Not for circulation outside the firm.

---

## Summary

- Aim: by the end of April 2027 each of you can run the analyst work on an engagement without close direction, and has the record to show it.
- Three parts: the work on live engagements, the Academy curriculum, and a monthly evaluation that ties them together.
- Time: two hours of protected learning every Friday, on your desk as "Learning hour".
- Outcome: from month three, an evaluation can recommend you for the firm's first consultant tier. That recommendation is earned on evidence, not time served.
- Supervision: Favour and Ruth report to Ezinne Orji. Abigail reports to Debo.

---

## 1. The six months

| Months | Stage | What you should be able to do by the end |
|---|---|---|
| November and December | Foundations | Keep an engagement record accurate without reminders. Minute a client meeting the same day. Raise a block early with a clear reason. |
| January and February | Ownership | Run a standing item end to end, including the follow-up. Prepare a meeting pack the engagement lead uses with light edits. Produce a first-draft analysis from client data. |
| March and April | Contribution | Own one defined piece of client work under the engagement lead, from brief to submitted draft. Brief a newer colleague on something you now know well. |

Each stage builds on the one before. Moving early is welcome when the evaluation shows it.

---

## 2. The Academy curriculum

You are enrolled in all five foundation courses on the platform under Academy. Take them in this order.

| By | Course | Hours | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 November | CFA Platform and Engagement | 10 | How engagements, tasks and deliverables run on the platform |
| 30 November | Professional Standards | 8 | Confidentiality, client conduct, how we write |
| 31 December | Core Consulting Skills | 24 | Structuring a problem, research, analysis, presenting |
| 31 January | Healthcare Fundamentals | 20 | How hospitals in this market work and where they break |
| 28 February | Financial Literacy for Healthcare | 16 | Reading a hospital's numbers: revenue, costs, cash |

- Pace: about one module a week fits inside the Friday learning hour.
- Use it: each Friday, write two lines on the Learning hour task saying one thing you learned and where it applies to a live engagement. That note matters more than the quiz score.
- After February: the firm will sponsor one specialist course for each of you, chosen with your supervisor to fit the engagements you are on.

---

## 3. How progress is measured

| Measure | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Quality of work | Tasks approved first time versus sent back, from the task history |
| Reliability | Tasks done on time, overdue, and blocks raised early |
| Learning | Academy modules completed against the dates above, and your Friday notes |
| Contribution | What the engagement leads say about your work on their projects |

Your supervisor reads this every month before your evaluation on the 28th. The evaluation scores five areas from 1 to 5. A 3 means doing the job well, and that is the expected score. You will hear the scores and the reasons in a conversation before anything is written down.

---

## 4. What we ask of you

- Keep your task statuses true. The record is how your progress becomes visible.
- Ask early. A question on day one costs nothing; the same question on the due date costs the deadline.
- Before each evaluation, send your supervisor three lines: what you delivered, what went wrong, what you want to learn next.
- Help each other. Teaching something is the fastest way to find out whether you know it.
