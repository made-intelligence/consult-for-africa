## 3. How we will review

### The Foundation's criteria come first.

We will score every file against the evaluation rubric the Foundation supplies, exactly as written, on TEFConnect. We will not substitute our own framework for yours. What we add is the discipline that makes a rubric produce the same answer whichever reviewer holds the file: a written guide to what "Yes" looks like for every question, worked examples at the boundary, and an evidence note against every score so that any result can be traced to the line of the application that earned it.

### Stage 1: preparing the first-level review.

Where the Foundation uses enumerators for the first, preliminary pass, we will train them on the agreed framework and criteria before they begin. Training covers the rubric question by question, a set of example applications pre-scored by our leads, and a short certification exercise. An enumerator who does not reach the agreement threshold is retrained before taking live files. We run the sessions in English, French and Arabic.

### Stage 2: expert application review.

Applications that pass the first level are read in full by an expert reviewer, who records a score and a one-line evidence note for each criterion. A reviewer working in English spends around 20 minutes on an application, and around 26 minutes in French or Arabic. We have budgeted that time deliberately. It is long enough to read the whole application and short enough to keep the queue moving.

### Component 2: business plan, financials and pitch video.

Shortlisted entrepreneurs submit a business plan, financial projections on the Foundation's template and a two to three minute pitch video. We review the three together, because the most useful question at this stage is whether they tell the same story.

| Element | What the reviewer tests | Minutes, English | Minutes, French or Arabic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business plan | Who the customer is and why they will pay; whether the plan is practical with the resources described; what the seed capital buys; the jobs and growth the plan can credibly create | 45 | 58 |
| Financial projections | That the numbers add up; that revenue follows from price and volume the plan describes; that costs include what it takes to deliver; cash in the first twelve months; whether the projections and the narrative agree | 30 | 39 |
| Pitch video | That the founder understands their own business and numbers; clarity, conviction and command; consistency with the written plan | 12 | 16 |

A plan whose financials assume a price the narrative never mentions, or a pitch that describes a different business from the plan, is noted and scored on that basis. We also flag, for the Foundation's decision rather than our own, files that appear templated, copied from another applicant or duplicated across accounts.

### Fairness to the applicant.

Reviewers score the business, not the prose. Our reviewer guide makes this explicit, and our calibration set deliberately includes strong businesses written plainly and weak businesses written well, so that reviewers learn to tell the difference before they score a live file.

### Re-review and correction.

Where the Foundation asks for a file to be re-reviewed, or an entrepreneur submits corrections, a second reviewer who did not score the original reads the corrected file against the original notes. Our price includes re-review of up to 10 per cent of Component 2 files, and further re-reviews are charged at the stated per-file rate.

## 4. Quality assurance and calibration

Our quality system runs before, during and after production, so that the ranked list holds up when the Foundation is asked how it was reached.

| When | What we do | What it catches |
|---|---|---|
| Before production | Every reviewer scores a calibration set of pre-scored applications in their language and must reach the agreement threshold to be certified | Reviewers who read the rubric differently from the leads |
| Throughout production | One file in ten is blind double scored by a second reviewer; disagreements beyond a set margin go to a lead for a third read | Individual scoring errors, before they reach the ranked list |
| Every week | Each reviewer's average score and pass rate are compared with the pool and with their own first week | Drift, fatigue, a reviewer becoming harsher or more lenient over time |
| Every week | A lead audits a sample of each reviewer's files, including every file near a selection boundary | Errors where they matter most |
| At the close | Scores are reconciled, outliers re-read, and the ranked lists signed off by the engagement director | A final list we can stand behind line by line |

A reviewer who drifts outside tolerance is paused, recalibrated on fresh examples and, if needed, their recent files are re-read. The Foundation sees these numbers every week.

## 7. Data protection and confidentiality

All review work is done on TEFConnect. We will not download, copy or hold applicant data outside the Foundation's platform. Every reviewer and enumerator signs a confidentiality undertaking before receiving access, and access is granted per person and withdrawn at the close of the engagement or the moment a person leaves the team.

All information we encounter in the course of the assignment remains the property of the Foundation, and we will not use or disclose it for any other purpose without the Foundation's written permission. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Any suspected breach is reported to the Foundation within 24 hours, with our account of what happened and what we have done about it. Our data and compliance lead owns these controls for the life of the engagement.
